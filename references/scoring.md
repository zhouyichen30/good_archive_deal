# Scoring Rubric (0–100)

Score every verified-live finalist. The score answers one question: **how good a buy is
this, right now, for this collector?** Underpricing dominates, but a cheap boring piece
should not outrank a fairly-priced grail — hence the other axes.

## The five axes

### 1. Value / underpricing — 40 pts
Compare the asking price (plus ~12% proxy/shipping overhead for Yahoo) against what the
piece type actually sells for. Use sold/closed comps where you have them; otherwise
estimate from comparable pieces.

- 36–40: priced well below sold comps (a real steal / mispriced listing).
- 28–35: clearly under market.
- 18–27: roughly fair market.
- 8–17: slightly high but not crazy.
- 0–7: overpriced / aspirational "troll" pricing.

Known Margiela Replica sold comps (rough, for calibration): leather jackets ~¥50,000 ·
trench/coats ~¥35,000 · pants ¥8,000–36,000 · cardigans ~¥20,000. So a ¥38,000 Line 14
Replica *leather* coat scores high on value (leather usually clears the trench comp).

### 2. Rarity / archive significance — 25 pts
- 21–25: iconic documented piece — `本人期` runway/collection piece, named archive motif
  (Margiela Stockman/trompe l'oeil/Artisanal; Issey A-POC/parachute/Tomio Mohri;
  CDG 88AW flower, 90s Homme Plus).
- 14–20: solid archive era with era tags, early period (`初期`), desirable season.
- 7–13: mainline from a normal season, no special provenance.
- 0–6: recent/basic/diffusion, little collector interest.

### 3. Condition — 15 pts
Read the Japanese description. `目立った傷や汚れなし` = no notable flaws (near top).
Deduct for `傷` (damage), `汚れ` (stains), `使用感` (heavy wear), detached tags, repairs.
- 13–15: excellent/deadstock. 8–12: good, minor wear. 4–7: visible flaws noted.
  0–3: significant damage.

### 4. Line desirability — 10 pts
- 9–10: Artisanal (`0`/`0 10`), Replica, mainline `10`, CDG Homme Plus / Junya.
- 6–8: standard mainline, CDG Homme/Homme Deux, Issey Miyake Men.
- 3–5: lower diffusion.
- 0–2: women's-leaning/licensed lines (`me`, MM6, Plantation) — usually a mismatch for
  this (male) collector unless unisex and desirable.

### 5. Buyability — 10 pts
- 9–10: verified live, 2+ days remaining, reasonable/free shipping, established seller
  rating.
- 5–8: live but ends soon (<24h) or higher shipping.
- 0–4: hard to buy, near-ending, or shipping/proxy overhead eats the deal.

## Bid count: treat it as a demand signal, and surface hot lots

Always capture the bid count (`入札 〜件`) when verifying an item, and show it in the
table. Read bids as **demand** — how much the market wants this piece. The user
specifically wants hot items with active bidding, so bidding activity is a **positive**
signal, not a penalty.

- **Many bids (5+)** → validated demand. The crowd agrees this is desirable, well
  described, and (for fungible markets like watches) likely authentic. Add **+5 to
  Value/desirability** and flag it as *hot* in the "why it's a good find" column.
- **Moderate bids (1–4)** → early interest building. Small positive, note it.
- **Zero bids** → no demand read yet. Judge purely on the piece's own merits (rarity,
  price, condition); neither bonus nor penalty.
- **即決 / Buy-It-Now** → no bidding to read; judge on price and merits alone.

**Sorting:** default sort is still by total score, but when the user asks for hot /
most-bid / trending items, sort by bid count (descending) and say so. A "Bids" column is
always shown so demand is visible at a glance.

One practical caution to state, not to penalize: a hot lot's current price is below where
it will close, and many Yahoo lots **auto-extend** (`自動延長`) when bid near the
deadline, so the real end time drifts later than listed. Bid early/high enough to matter,
and treat the current price as a floor.

## Bands

- **80–100** — genuine BUY flag. Lead with these.
- **65–79** — worth a look; include and note the caveat holding it back.
- **< 65** — usually omit from the Top 20 unless the pool is thin.

## Worked examples

**Line 14 Replica leather coat, ¥38,000, live, 1 day left, good condition**
Value 34 (leather under the ~¥50k leather comp) · Rarity 18 (Replica line, documented
provenance) · Condition 12 · Line 10 · Buyability 7 (ends soon) → **81 = BUY**, flag the
short clock.

**KOKONOE Line 10 cotton knit, ¥15,800, live, 5 days, minor wear**
Value 30 (cheap for `本人期`-adjacent knit) · Rarity 15 (early `ここのえ` era) ·
Condition 10 · Line 8 · Buyability 9 → **72 = worth a look**, great low-cost entry.

**Recent `me ISSEY MIYAKE` pleated blouse, ¥6,000, live**
Value 20 · Rarity 5 · Condition 12 · Line 2 (women's diffusion) · Buyability 9 →
**48 = omit** for this collector despite the low price.

## Presentation

Sort the Top 20 by total score descending. Show the score in its own column so the user
sees *why* it ranks. In the standout bullets, name the axis doing the work ("cheapest
entry into real archive," "documented `本人期` grail priced fairly").
