# The Watchlist — fixed default roster

This is the user's curated brand list. **Every default / multi-brand run pulls exactly
these ten brands, top 10 each, men's only.** Don't substitute or drop brands unless the
user names a different scope for that run. For each brand: search the primary terms, then
**rank by the Focus list**, **exclude the Avoid list**, keep within the Budget as
context, and order brands by Tier when trimming.

Tiers: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ / Tier A = lead with these; Tier A− = include but lower; "future" = only
when the user raises the budget.

Prices below are USD (÷150 for ¥). Budgets are guidance, not hard cutoffs — flag a
standout just outside range, but don't fill a top 10 with over-budget pieces.

---

## 1. Issey Miyake Men (1978–1999) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
The best fit. Search: `ISSEY MIYAKE MEN`, `イッセイミヤケメン`, add era/`筆タグ` (brush
tag = 80s–90s), `Plantation`, `IS` / `i.s.`.
- **Focus:** Leather Jacket (like the 99AW), Garment-Dyed Coat (`製品染め`), Pin-tucked
  Shirt (`ピンタック`), Plantation-era pieces, Natural Wrinkle (`シワ加工`), Hand-knit
  (`手編み`), 3D Knit (`3Dニット`, `立体`), Linen Tailoring (`リネン`), Permanent Wrinkle,
  Sports Jacket.
- **Avoid:** Pleats Please (women's), Bao Bao (`バオバオ`), plain logo tees.
- **Budget:** $300–1,200 (¥45k–180k).

## 2. Maison / Martin Margiela (1989–2008) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Archive era, **NOT** the modern Glam Slam / bag-driven line. Search: `マルタンマルジェラ`,
`マルジェラ 10`, `マルジェラ 14`, `アーティザナル`, `レプリカ`.
- **Focus:** ⑩ Mens (Line 10), ⑭ Wardrobe (Line 14), Miss Deanna knit, H-Line coat,
  Artisanal (`0`/`0 10`, budget permitting), Replica Military, Painted pieces (`ペンキ`,
  `ペイント`).
- **Key years to prioritize:** AW99, SS01, AW03, AW04, SS06.
- **Avoid:** Glam Slam bags/era, MM6 women's, recent mainline logo pieces.
- **Budget:** $400–3,000 (¥60k–450k).

## 3. Yohji Yamamoto Pour Homme (1988–2005) — Tier A
Black, loose, no logo, but architecturally strong cut. Search: `ヨウジヤマモト プールオム`,
`Yohji Yamamoto POUR HOMME`.
- **Focus:** Wool Gabardine (`ウールギャバ`), Double Coat / double-breasted, Asymmetrical
  Blazer (`アシンメトリー`), Long Shirt (`ロングシャツ`), Balloon Pant (`バルーン`).
- **Avoid:** loud graphics/logo pieces, recent Ground Y / collab hype.
- **Budget:** no hard cap — favor strong-cut tailoring/outerwear over tees.

## 4. Helmut Lang (1996–2004) — the Lang era only
Pre-2005, Helmut's own house (not the post-2005 relaunch). Search: `ヘルムートラング`,
`HELMUT LANG`, `本人期`.
- **Focus:** Astro Jacket, Leather Blazer, Painter Jeans (`ペインター`), Bondage Strap
  (`ボンデージ`), M-69 Cargo.
- **Avoid:** post-2005 relaunch pieces, basic logo tees.

## 5. Dries Van Noten (90s) — the textiles, not the florals-as-print
Search: `ドリスヴァンノッテン`, `DRIES VAN NOTEN`, `アーカイブ`.
- **Focus:** Floral Jacquard (`ジャガード` — woven, not printed), Military Tailoring, Silk
  Shirts (`シルク`), Wool Coat (`ウールコート`).
- **Note:** prize the *woven fabric*, not surface floral prints.

## 6. Comme des Garçons Homme Plus (1995–2004) — Tier A−
The tailored/archive window, not recent dramatic Kawakubo runway. Search:
`コムデギャルソン オムプリュス`, `HOMME PLUS`, `AD199x`/`AD200x`.
- **Focus:** 90s–early-00s tailoring, leather, era-tagged jackets/shirts.
- **Avoid:** very recent, highly theatrical mainline collections.

## 7. Lemaire (Christophe Lemaire era) — modern, will become archive
Search: `ルメール`, `LEMAIRE`.
- **Focus:** Twisted Pants (`ツイスト`), Wrap Coat (`ラップコート`), Leather Blouson.

## 8. Jil Sander (pre-Raf, 1990s)
The 90s minimalist run before Raf Simons. Search: `ジルサンダー`, `JIL SANDER`.
- **Focus:** Leather, Cashmere Coat (`カシミヤ`), Minimal Suit.
- **Avoid:** Raf-era and later, +J / Uniqlo collab.

## 9. Carol Christian Poell (CCP) — future / higher budget
Only when the budget rises; still surface standouts. Search: `カールクリスチャンポエル`,
`CAROL CHRISTIAN POELL`, `CCP`.
- **Focus:** Object-Dyed Leather, Tornado Zip, Prosthetic/Drip boots.

## 10. Unkruid (2023–, Antwerp) — modern, new-brand watch
Belgian, founded 2023 (first collection 2024SS), small-scale production with each garment
hand-numbered. Natural-fibre cloth from artisan mills, chosen for how it ages through wear
and washing. Search: `unkruid`, `アンクルイド`, `UNKRUID`.
- **Focus:** Shepherds Coat (waxed cotton canvas, and the wool/alpaca loden version),
  Sunday Best Jacket, Late Night Jacket (corduroy / coutil), baggy pants, wool pullovers,
  Dream caps (`キャップ`).
- **Avoid:** nothing brand-specific yet — just the standard women's/exclusion filters.
  Watch for misspellings and unrelated Dutch-language "onkruid" listings.
- **Budget:** no resale comps exist yet. Retail anchors it — the Shepherds Coat is ~$1,800
  at Neighbour — so treat anything meaningfully under retail as the signal, and expect to
  judge on retail delta rather than archive scarcity.
- **Reality check:** this is a two-year-old label, not archive. Secondary supply on Yahoo
  Auctions is near zero most days, and a zero-result run for Unkruid is an honest result,
  not a missed search. Report the count and move on rather than padding with lookalikes.
  Japanese distribution runs through V.O.F / VISION OF FASHION (CONTEXT TOKYO, Otokage in
  Kyoto, kune in Iwate, N id Tokyo), so what does surface tends to come out of those.
