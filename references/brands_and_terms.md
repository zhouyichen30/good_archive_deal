# Brands, Search Terms & Dating Cheat Sheet

## Target brands and Japanese search keywords

Search Yahoo Auctions in Japanese for far better results. Combine a brand term with a
garment term (e.g. `マルジェラ レプリカ ジャケット`).

**Core roster:**

Unkruid is the odd one out here: founded 2023, so there are no era names and almost no
secondary supply. Search the plain brand terms (both spellings — sellers use the roman
`unkruid` at least as often as `アンクルイド`) and expect a small or empty result set. Do
not pad it with visually similar Belgian/pastoral brands.

Era/period names (`〜期`) are how Japanese sellers title archive pieces — they're the
highest-signal keywords for these brands after the brand name itself. The ones above came
from real listings, so they match how sellers actually write.

For a "per-brand top 10 / all brands" run, iterate the whole roster. These names are broad,
so lean on `メンズ` (men's) and garment/`アーカイブ` terms to cut noise, and always apply
the men's + exclusion filters below.

## Garment terms

- Jacket/blazer: `ジャケット` · Coat: `コート` · Blouson: `ブルゾン`
- Pants/trousers: `パンツ`, `スラックス` · Denim: `デニム`
- Tee: `Tシャツ`, `カットソー` · Shirt: `シャツ` · Knit/sweater: `ニット`, `セーター`
- Setup/suit: `セットアップ`, `スーツ`

## Accessory terms

Accessories are in scope. Search them the same way — brand term + accessory term, one
search per pair, plain keywords only.

- Bag: `バッグ` (also `トートバッグ` tote, `ショルダーバッグ` shoulder, `クラッチ` clutch)
- Ring: `リング`, `指輪` · Bracelet: `ブレスレット`, `バングル` (bangle)
- Necklace: `ネックレス` · Wallet: `財布` · Belt: `ベルト`
- Watch/eyewear if surfaced: `時計`, `眼鏡`/`サングラス`

Accessories that carry real archive value tend to be the house's signature hardware —
Margiela's Line 11 jewelry (four-stitch rings, screw/bolt bracelets, the "invisible"
ring), Yohji's silver 950 pieces (e.g. BROTHER-movie bracelets), CDG accessories, and
Issey hardware. A generic branded pouch is not a grail; judge accessories on the same
rarity/value axes as clothing, not on brand name alone.

## Exclusion terms (filter these OUT unless requested)

`レディース` (women's) · `ワンピース` (dress) · `スカート` (skirt) ·
`スニーカー` (sneakers) · `足袋`/`タビ` boots · `香水` (fragrance). Note
diffusion/women's-leaning lines: `me ISSEY MIYAKE`, `PLEATS PLEASE` (mostly women's),
`MM6` (mostly women's), `Plantation`.

Bags, rings, bracelets, necklaces, belts, and wallets are **in scope** — they used to be
excluded, but accessories are now part of the hunt. Still drop women's-marked accessories
unless the user says otherwise, since much archive jewelry is unisex but bags often skew.

## Signal terms that indicate value (rank with these — do NOT search with them)

Use these to *score* candidates you already pulled. Putting them in the query itself
collapses the result pool, because most sellers don't put them in the title — a search
for `ISSEY MIYAKE MEN アーカイブ 本人期` returned only 3 items where the plain
`ISSEY MIYAKE MEN` search returned 50.


- `本人期` — "the man's own era" (designed while the founder still led the house). Big
  collector premium for Margiela pre-2009, Issey pre-2000s, etc.
- `アーカイブ` / `archive` · `希少` / `レア` / `激レア` — rare · `初期` — early period
- `名作` — masterpiece/famous piece · `deadstock` / `新品タグ付` — new with tags
- Margiela quality-tag era codes (see below) · CDG `AD####` year codes.

## Margiela tag / era dating cheat sheet

Margiela pieces carry a numeric quality tag and an era-specific distributor tag.

**Quality-tag season codes** (examples): `991` = 1999SS · `0012` = 2001AW ·
`2010 02` = 2010AW. Two digits = year, trailing digit(s) = season half.

**Distributor / agency tags by era (Japan):**
- 企業コード — 1996AW–1998SS
- オリゾンティ (Orizzonti) — 1998AW–2001AW
- KOKONOE (roman) — 2000AW–2001AW
- ここのえ (Kokonoe, kana) — 2002SS–2011AW  ← very common on desirable 本人期 pieces
- スタッフインターナショナル (Staff International) — 2011AW–2017AW
- マルジェラジャパン (Margiela Japan) — 2018SS–present

Rule of thumb: `ここのえ` + `10` (menswear line) + low price = strong archive candidate.

## Line numbers (the circled/number system)

- `0` / `0 10` = Artisanal (hand-remade, French-made, highest value)
- `10` = men's mainline · `14` = men's basics/Replica-adjacent · `4`/`14` = Replica
- `6` = MM6 (mostly women's) · `22` = shoes

## Margiela "Replica" line (what the user specifically wants)

A real capsule (Lines 4 & 14) since 1994: found vintage garments faithfully reproduced,
carrying a **second internal tag** stating the Style / Provenance / Period of the
original. This is distinct from "replica = fake," and distinct from the famous Replica
GAT sneakers. Look for `レプリカ` in titles on jackets, leather, coats, pants, knits.
